        🌱 Energy-Saving Tips

        💡

        Switch to LED

        LED bulbs give the same light for a fraction of the power of incandescent or CFL lamps and last far longer.

        ❄️

        Use the AC wisely

        Set a moderate temperature, clean the filters regularly and pair it with a ceiling fan to feel cooler.

        🔌

        Cut standby power

        Chargers, TVs and set-top boxes draw power even when idle. Use a switch strip and turn them off.

        🧊

        Look after the fridge

        Keep it away from heat, do not overfill it, let hot food cool first and check the door seal.

        ⏰

        Batch heavy tasks

        Run the iron, washing machine and water pump in batches instead of in many short sessions.

        ⭐

        Buy efficient

        Choose inverter ACs and appliances with good efficiency ratings; they cost more upfront but save every month.

        ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

        How is the bill calculated?

        Each appliance's monthly energy is Wattage × Quantity × Hours/day × Days × Duty ÷ 1000 (in kWh). The total kWh is priced with your flat rate or slabs, then any fixed charge and VAT are added.

        What is a slab (tiered) tariff?

        Instead of one price for every unit, the first block of units has a lower rate, the next block a higher rate, and so on. The more you use, the higher the rate on the extra units.

        Is the result exact?

        No, it is an estimate. Real bills depend on actual usage, meter readings, current tariff, demand charges and any late fees. Check slab rates against your distributor's latest notice.

        Is my data saved or uploaded?

        Your appliances, settings, bill history and splitter are stored only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server. The share link packs only your appliances and tariff into the link itself.

        Can I install it or use it offline?

        Yes. SmartWatt is a Progressive Web App: use your browser's “Install” or “Add to Home Screen” option to use it like an app.
